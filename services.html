Most Established Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma Therapy (PRP)

PRP is a concentration of platelets derived from the patient's own blood, rich in growth factors that accelerate tissue repair and reduce inflammation. It is the most established regenerative therapy in veterinary practice, with the strongest clinical evidence base and the widest range of applications.

Because PRP is derived from the patient's own blood, there is no risk of immune rejection — making it a safe, minimally invasive option for a wide range of conditions.

Conditions Treated

Equine tendon injuries Ligament damage Joint disease Wound healing Canine osteoarthritis Soft tissue injuries
📊
Clinical Evidence: PRP therapy achieves 85% recovery rates in equine athletes with tendon and ligament injuries, and significantly reduces inflammation in companion animal joint disease cases.

Advanced Cell Therapy

Mesenchymal Stem Cell Therapy (MSC)

MSC therapy uses autologous stem cells — harvested from the patient's own adipose tissue or bone marrow — processed and administered to target sites of chronic injury or disease. Stem cells have the unique ability to differentiate into specialised tissue cells and to modulate the immune response, promoting natural healing.

This therapy is particularly effective for chronic, difficult-to-treat conditions where conventional treatments have failed to deliver lasting results.

Conditions Treated

Chronic osteoarthritis Tendon repair Musculoskeletal disease Canine joint disease Equine lameness Immune-mediated conditions
📊
Clinical Evidence: MSC therapies achieve up to 75% efficacy in osteoarthritis cases, with dogs showing 70% pain reduction within weeks of treatment. Recovery times are reduced by up to 50% compared to traditional surgery.

Emerging Frontier

Extracellular Vesicle Therapy (EV)

Extracellular vesicles are nano-sized particles secreted by stem cells, carrying bioactive molecules — proteins, lipids, and RNA — that mediate cell-to-cell communication. EV therapy delivers the therapeutic benefits of stem cells without the complexity of live cell administration.

As an emerging modality, EV therapy represents the frontier of regenerative medicine — and VETarTo's founder has direct research experience in this field from seven years at REMEDI, University of Galway.

Applications

Inflammation modulation Tissue repair Immune regulation Chronic disease management Post-surgical recovery
📊
Emerging Science: The global extracellular vesicle market is valued at $229.3M (2025) with a 23% CAGR to 2033 — reflecting rapid growth in clinical confidence and research investment in this therapy.
